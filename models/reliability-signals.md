# Reliability Signals

## Purpose

Reliability signals are the telemetry patterns that help teams understand service health and predict failure.

## Example Signals

- latency trend
- error rate trend
- saturation trend
- queue depth
- timeout rate
- deployment failure rate
- correlation strength

## How To Use

- connect each signal to a service objective
- review signals in context instead of isolation
- identify which signals are early warning indicators

## Use

Use these signals as the first layer of predictive observability, not as isolated metrics with no service context.
