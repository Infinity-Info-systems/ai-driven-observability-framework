# SLO And SLI Template

## Service

`[Service name]`

## Objective

Define the service outcome the team cares about.

## SLI

- metric name
- measurement method
- target level

## SLO

- target percentage or threshold
- review cadence
- error budget note
- service owner

## Notes

- what the metric means
- what action happens when it degrades

## Use

Use this template to connect observability signals to a real service outcome and an accountable owner.
